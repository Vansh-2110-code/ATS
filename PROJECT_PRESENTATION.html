Workflow Automation

Candidate Pipeline & Lifecycle Engine

Standardized multi-stage candidate conversion funnel with built-in audit trails and 30-day recruiter ownership locks.

1

Sourcing & Intake

Candidates imported via Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Walk-Ins, or Excel batch files. Automatic duplicate checks perform phone and email verification.

2

First Call & Screening (L1)

Recruiters assess communication rating, salary expectation, and location compatibility. Automated score calculation determines eligibility.

3

Client Submission & Second Round (L2)

Profiles submitted to client HR. Team Leaders review candidate status and conduct second-round verification for client interviews.

4

Offer & Documentation

Post-offer tracking monitors offer release, salary negotiation, Aadhar/PAN document verification, and joining availability date confirmation.

5

Joined & Financial Trigger

Once marked as Joined, candidate data auto-syncs with the Finance module to generate placement revenue and invoice drafts.
